Who we are

What this site collects

Nothing that we keep. This site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sets no cookies.

Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and the page you asked for, so it can run the site and keep it safe.

The subscribe box

If you type your email address into the subscribe box, your browser sends it straight to Substack to fill in its subscribe form. This site never receives it or stores it.

Substack

When you subscribe, Substack holds your email address and handles any payment, under its own privacy policy. As the publisher, we can see who subscribes on Substack.

The pictures beside our latest pieces load from Substack's servers, so Substack sees your browser ask for them. To stop getting our emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them.

Other sites

Links to Substack, LinkedIn and other sites take you to services we do not run. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes

If what this site does with information changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
