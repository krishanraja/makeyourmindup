AI news for the creative industries, every morning. Every day the Edition at makeyourmindup.ai brings the AI stories that change how work in the creative, arts, music, entertainment, media and sports industries is made, sold and paid for. Each piece takes one story and asks why, five times, until the money is in plain sight.

One email every Saturday brings you the week's pieces and the creative-industry news that matters.

**The house rules.** Plain English. If jargon sneaks in, we translate it on the spot. No sermons: we show our working, and you make your mind up. Every number names whoever produced it. British spelling, obviously.

**Who makes it.** We write every piece, read the audio edition and front the Shorts. A blind panel of AI judges checks every draft, then a human makes the final call.

Every written piece is free to read, in full.
