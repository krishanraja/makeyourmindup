# Paid-tier copy, ready to paste

The plans, as ruled on 2026-11-19 (see the media kit, "Free and paid"). Each plan includes everything in the one
above it.

| Plan | Gets |
|---|---|
| Monthly | The working files, the audio editions, and one brain module of their choice, from the second monthly payment. |
| Annual | Everything monthly gets, plus the monthly live session and the Real-or-Theatre Desk: one AI claim a month, sent in by reply, returned as a private written teardown. |
| Founding | Everything annual gets, plus every brain module free and one check-in a month, booked by reply when they want it, to unpick their biggest AI bottlenecks. |

The files sit on the members page; every other perk is claimed by replying, and brain modules come with a one-time
code. `[MEMBERS CODE]` is filled in at paste time from the Vercel setting, never written here. What has to exist
first is in `docs/plans/2026-11-19-paid-tier-fulfilment.md`. The copy
names neither the company behind the brain modules nor its site, and names no person. No price appears in it.

## Welcome email to paid subscribers

Substack sends one paid welcome to monthly and annual subscribers alike, so it describes both.

Subject: Thank you, properly.

Every written piece on makeyourmindup is free, and it stays free. So you've just paid for something you could have read for nothing. That is either a vote of confidence or a billing error, and we are choosing to treat it as the first. Thank you.

Every paid plan gets:

- The diagrams and artifacts from every piece, as files you can take and reuse, at makeyourmindup.ai/members with the code [MEMBERS CODE].
- The audio editions.
- One brain module of your choice: a small piece of working software that checks a number, a board update or an email against your own files before you rely on it. On the monthly plan it arrives with your second payment.

The annual plan also gets two things. A live session with us, once a month. And the Real-or-Theatre Desk: once a month, send us one AI claim you are being asked to believe, such as a vendor pitch, a board slide or a press release, and it comes back to you as a written teardown, every part stamped real or theatre.

To claim the rest, hit reply. Ask for the list of brain modules and we'll send you a code for yours, or paste in the claim for the Desk. We read every reply.

If something here hasn't reached you yet, it's still at the printers, and it lands in your inbox the day it's ready.

In the spirit of follow.the.money, here's where your money goes. It pays the running costs of the machinery behind this, so the machines do the grind and we get to spend our time on the thinking and the writing.
