# Unverified claims in the dry run pitches

**Every number marked CLAIM in `PITCHES.md` came from a search result and is
unverified.**

That is deliberate rather than sloppy. In this channel a contested number is the
subject, not the evidence. The whole point of follow.the.money is tracing a figure
back to whoever produced it and finding out what it counts.

**The rule:** production checks each one against the primary source before a
word gets written. Anything that does not survive gets cut, or reframed into a
piece about how fast an unverified number travelled, which is a different and
often better piece.

**Nothing in this folder is publishable as it stands.** These are pitches. They
have not been through the kill list on their finished text, they have not been
through the panel, and they are not briefs.

## The claims

| Pitch | The unverified figure |
|---|---|
| `bill0` Canva | That Canva pulled its 2026 revenue growth forecast to around 20 per cent after AI costs ran past plan, on roughly 220 million users and 2.65bn of revenue. Aggregator reporting, not a primary filing. |
| `bill1` OpenAI | That OpenAI loses 1.35 dollars for every dollar it earns. A very large, very quotable number with a thin paper trail, which is the shape of subject the format exists for. |
| `bill2` gross margin | Needs a real or credibly constructed mid-market P&L. If it is constructed it must say so loudly, in a channel built on straight evidence. |
| `gap1` hidden use | That a third of UK workers hide their AI use, from one survey. Other studies put it higher, and the spread between them is the actual story. |

If a figure here is repeated anywhere without its producer named, the Fact
Checker holds an absolute veto over it. That judge never calibrates down.
